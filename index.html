🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Invotix is built with privacy at its core. Your data belongs to you — we never see it, share it, or sell it.

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • Cloud backups go to YOUR own Google Drive (hidden folder)
  • We have no servers that store your business data
  • We cannot access, read, or modify your data
  • You can revoke Google Drive access anytime
