1. Introduction

Invotix is an invoice management app developed by Luminex Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

All your business data is stored locally on your device. We take your privacy seriously.

By using Invotix, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and contact us at labsluminex@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Business profile data including name, address, email, phone, tax ID, logo, and signature
  • Client information including names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses
  • Invoice data including items, quantities, amounts, dates, taxes, discounts, and payment status
  • Payment records including amounts, methods, references, and dates
  • Bank account details including account number, IBAN, and SWIFT code
  • Product and service catalog including names, prices, descriptions, and stock quantities
  • Notes, payment terms, and custom text entered by you

Information collected automatically:

  • Device model, operating system version, app version, and build number
  • A unique device identifier used for backup sync
  • App usage analytics collected through Firebase Analytics
  • Crash reports and diagnostics collected through Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising identifiers collected by Google AdMob

Information from third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In provides your name, email address, and profile photo
  • Google Drive is accessed only for backup and restore of your app data
  • ExchangeRate-API provides currency conversion rates without receiving your personal data
  • Google AdMob collects device and advertising data to serve ads
  • Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage data
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic data

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide invoice creation, client management, payment tracking, and business management
  • Generate PDF invoices, reports, and data exports
  • Back up and restore your data using your Google Drive account
  • Schedule and display local notifications for payment reminders
  • Fetch currency exchange rates for conversion
  • Track app usage patterns to improve features through Firebase Analytics
  • Monitor and fix crashes through Firebase Crashlytics
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Process premium feature purchases through Google Play

4. Data Storage & Security

All your business data is stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database. Luminex Labs does not operate a server that stores your business records.

If you use the backup feature, files are stored in your own Google Drive account. We do not keep copies on our servers.

All connections to Google services use HTTPS/TLS encryption. Images such as logos and signatures are stored locally as base64-encoded data.

You have full control over your data. You can create, view, edit, delete, and export all records from within the app.

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data.

Your data is shared only when:

Analytics data collected is anonymized or aggregated where possible.

10. How to Delete Your Data

You have three ways to delete your data:

Method 1 — Delete individual records inside the app

You can delete any individual client, invoice, payment, item, bank account, or business profile directly from within the app. Open the record you want to remove and use the delete option. This permanently removes that record from your local database.

Method 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Invotix from your device permanently deletes all locally stored data including all business profiles, clients, invoices, payments, items, bank accounts, settings, and preferences. This action cannot be undone.

Method 3 — Delete Google Drive backups from inside the app

If you have created backups using Google Drive, you can delete them from within the app. Go to Settings → Backup & Restore, where you will see a list of available backups. You can delete each backup individually from this screen. This permanently removes the backup file from your Google Drive account.

You can also delete backup files directly from your Google Drive account by opening Google Drive and removing files from the Invotix backup folder.

If you need help with data deletion, contact labsluminex@gmail.com and we will respond within 48 hours.

12. Data Retention

  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.
  • Google Drive backups remain until you delete them (see section 10).
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained per Google's data retention policy.
  • Crashlytics data is retained per Firebase's policy.
  • AdMob data is retained per Google's advertising data policy.
  • Deletion requests via email are confirmed within 48 hours.

All transfers are protected by the respective service provider's privacy policies and applicable data protection frameworks.
