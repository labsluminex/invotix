1. Introduction & Overview

APP_NAME is an invoice management app developed by Luminex Labs.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, how it is used, and what choices you have.

This policy applies to the mobile app, optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup features, and the public legal web pages.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Business profile data such as business name, address, email, phone number, tax ID, logo, signature, notes, invoice terms, and custom text
  • Client and customer information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, and billing or shipping addresses
  • Invoice data such as items, quantities, dates, totals, statuses, taxes, discounts, and payment terms
  • Payment records such as paid amounts, methods, references, and dates
  • Bank account details such as account number, IBAN, SWIFT, and related payment information
  • Product or service catalog data such as names, prices, stock quantities, and descriptions

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version, app version, and build number
  • A device identifier used to help backup, restore, and future sync features work correctly
  • Future: anonymous usage analytics through Firebase Analytics
  • Future: crash logs and diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics

Information from third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In may provide your name, email address, and profile photo
  • Google Drive is used only for backup and restore in your own Google account
  • ExchangeRate-API provides exchange rate data and does not receive your invoice or client data
  • Future: AdMob may collect advertising and device identifiers
  • Future: Firebase Analytics may collect anonymous usage events

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide invoice creation, business management, client management, and payment tracking
  • Generate PDFs, exports, reports, and analytics
  • Save, restore, and organize your local data
  • Back up and restore your data using your Google Drive account
  • Schedule and display local notifications for reminders and alerts
  • Fetch exchange rates for currency conversion
  • Future: improve app quality and feature decisions through analytics
  • Future: diagnose bugs and crashes
  • Future: display advertising in the free tier
  • Future: process premium feature purchases

4. Data Storage & Security

All core app data is stored locally on your device using a local database. Luminex Labs does not operate a server-side database for your business data at this time.

If you use backup and restore, backup files are stored in your own Google Drive account. We do not keep a copy on our own servers.

Data sent to third-party services such as Google APIs is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Images such as business logos and signatures are stored locally in base64 form inside app data.

You control your data. You can create, edit, delete, and export your records from inside the app.

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not share your business data with third parties except where sharing happens because of your own action or because it is necessary for a feature to work.

  • Google Drive backup or restore initiated by you
  • Sending invoices, PDFs, or exports through apps selected by you
  • Compliance with valid legal obligations or court orders

Future analytics will be limited to anonymized or aggregated information where possible.

Users in the EEA, UK, California, or similar jurisdictions may have additional legal rights under applicable privacy laws.

10. Children’s Privacy

APP_NAME is a business app and is not designed for children under 13. It is also not designed for children under 16 where GDPR-related age thresholds apply.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided personal information through the app, we will take steps to delete it as soon as reasonably possible.

11. Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device while the app is installed unless you delete it earlier.

If you uninstall the app, local app data may be removed from your device according to platform behavior. Google Drive backups remain in your Drive until you delete them.

Future analytics and crash data, when added, will follow the retention policies of the relevant Google or Firebase service.

12. International Data Transfers

Because third-party services such as Google and ExchangeRate-API may operate globally, some limited data connected to those services may be processed outside your country.

Any such processing is handled under the policies and safeguards of those third-party providers.
